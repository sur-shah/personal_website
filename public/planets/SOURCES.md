Planet surface maps from NASA/JPL's Solar System Simulator texture library:

- Earth: https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/earth.html (JPL/Caltech/USGS)
- Mars: https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/mars.html (Viking imagery, JPL/Caltech/USGS)
- Saturn: https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/saturn.html (representative, fictional atmosphere by Don Davis for JPL/Caltech)

The textures are used as visual assets, not scientific models.
